Finally, the day of the competition arrived. Many students gathered in the school hall to watch the event. The teacher asked the contestants several questions. John answered most of the questions correctly because he had prepared well. Peter, on the other hand, could not answer many questions because he had not studied enough. He became worried and regretted wasting his time.

At the end of the competition, John was announced as the winner. Everybody clapped for him, and the teacher presented him with his prize. Peter felt ashamed of himself and went to congratulate his friend. He apologised for laughing at John and promised to change his attitude towards his studies. John forgave him and encouraged him to work harder.

that day, Peter learnt to be patient and hardworking. He understood that success did not come through laziness or overconfidence. He began to study regularly and improved his performance in school. This experience taught everyone an important lesson: we should never give up, even when progress is slow, because patience and hard work lead to success. Indeed, slow and steady wins the race.
